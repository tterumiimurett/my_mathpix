# Publish the paid product under an organization

Development and TestFlight validation may begin under the owner's individual Apple Developer membership, but the public paid product will be published under an organization membership. This adds legal-entity, D-U-N-S, domain, website, and enrollment work before launch, but avoids presenting the owner's personal legal name as the seller and establishes a durable identity for subscriptions, support, privacy obligations, and future collaborators.
